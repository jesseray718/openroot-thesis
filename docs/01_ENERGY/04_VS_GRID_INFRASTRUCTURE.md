## Comparative Analysis

All AeroCement values are theoretical targets requiring validation.

---

## Economic Analysis

At $0.12/kWh grid electricity and 3,500 kWh/year cooling savings:

```
Annual savings = 3,500 × $0.12 = $420/year
10-year savings = $4,200
20-year savings = $8,400
```

Against materials cost target of $10,000–15,000 for complete dome shell:  
**Simple payback on cooling alone: 12–18 years.**

With food production value (see [Life Support](../03_LIFE/README.md)), payback shortens considerably.
