# Quail Towers — Section 5.2

> ⚠️ **THEORETICAL** — Individual quail stocking densities are within industry norms. The integration of quail towers with dome thermal mass and aquaponics represents the novel system architecture.

*From THESIS.md Section 5.2*

---

## Why Coturnix Quail (*Coturnix coturnix japonica*)

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Feed conversion ratio | 2.5:1 (feed:egg by weight) |
| Egg production | 250–300 eggs/year per hen |
| Space requirement | 0.05–0.1 m²/bird (>50× more efficient than chickens) |
| Maturation | 6–8 weeks to laying age |
| Noise | Minimal (important for urban) |
| Disease resistance | Robust |

---

## Tower Design

Vertical stacking of quail cages along the dome's south wall provides:

1. **Passive heating** — Metabolic heat (≈5W/bird × 100 birds = 500W) in winter
2. **CO₂ enrichment** — For attached greenhouse/aquaponics zone
3. **Nutrient cycling** — High-nitrogen liquid waste → aquaponics input
4. **Protein production** — In minimal footprint

---

## Production Targets (50 m² Tower, 500–1,000 Birds)

| Output | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Eggs | 350–700/day |
| Meat (culled males/excess) | 2–4 kg/week |
| Liquid waste for aquaponics | 50–100 L/day |

---

## Integration Notes

Quail metabolic heat is a meaningful supplement to dome heating in winter (500W = ~0.4 kWh thermal/hour). Waste nitrogen connects to aquaponics nitrogen cycle — see [Aquaponics](03_AQUAPONICS.md).
