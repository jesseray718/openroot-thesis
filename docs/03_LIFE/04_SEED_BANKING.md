## Seed Sovereignty

The ability to reproduce the entire food system from open-pollinated seed is a **core design requirement.** The AeroCement system does not rely on hybrid or patented seed stock.

---

## Underground Vault Design

Location: 1.5m depth (uses ground temperature stability for free temperature control)

**Storage Conditions (Target):**

| Parameter | Target |
|-----------|--------|
| Temperature | 8–12°C (ground-coupled) |
| Humidity | <40% RH (sealed mylar + desiccant) |
| Viability target | >80% germination at 5 years |

---

## Recommended Seed Categories

**Food crops (open-pollinated varieties):**
- Tomato, pepper, squash, bean, corn, sunflower
- Kale and brassicas
- Root vegetables (carrot, beet, turnip)
- Culinary herbs

**Priority traits:**
- Heat tolerance
- Drought resistance
- Landrace/heritage varieties adapted to local conditions

**Cover crops and green manure:**
- Legumes for nitrogen fixation
- Grasses for carbon addition

---

## Seed Bank Maintenance

- Test germination rates annually (10-seed test sample per variety)
- Replenish from each season's harvest
- Document variety name, source, date stored, germination test results
- **Token issuance:** Each verified variety deposited earns SEED tokens (see [Economy](../04_ECONOMY/README.md))
