## AeroCement Defined

AeroCement is the working name for a lightweight geopolymer composite proposed as the primary structural and thermal material. It is not a single patented material but a class of formulations sharing:
- Fly ash or metakaolin as alumino-silicate precursor
- Alkaline activator (NaOH + Na₂SiO₃ solution)
- Lightweight aggregate (perlite, pumice, or recycled expanded glass)
- Fiber reinforcement (basalt fiber or recycled PET)

## Geopolymer Chemistry

```
Al-Si source + Alkaline solution → [SiO₄]⁴⁻ + [AlO₄]⁵⁻ monomers
                                          ↓
                              Polycondensation
                                          ↓
                         3D aluminosilicate polymer network
```

Geopolymer chemistry is well-established (Davidovits 1991; Provis & van Deventer 2009). The specific formulation targeting 0.3–0.6 W/m·K with structural adequacy requires iterative mix design and testing.

---

## Ferrocement Fallback

For sites without fly ash access, ferrocement (1:1.5 cement:sand, 2-layer chicken wire mesh) is a proven alternative. Ferrocement domes have been built successfully since the 1960s (Nervi, Waterhouse). AeroCement improves on ferrocement for thermal performance but ferrocement provides adequate structural performance.

See [MATERIAL_SPECIFICATIONS.md](MATERIAL_SPECIFICATIONS.md) for both mix designs.
