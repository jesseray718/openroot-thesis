# Urban Applications — Section 4.3

> ⚠️ **THEORETICAL** — Urban deployment configurations are proposed designs. Structural loads on existing buildings must be verified by a qualified structural engineer before any rooftop installation.

*From THESIS.md Section 4.3*

---

## Three Deployment Contexts

### 1. Urban Rooftop Modules

6–9m dome units placed on existing flat-roof structures in dense urban areas.
- 28–64 m² conditioned living space per unit
- Thermal mass benefit to building below
- **Critical:** Existing roof load capacity must be verified by structural engineer. AeroCement dome shell weight ≈ 15,000–20,000 kg for 6m diameter.

### 2. Suburban Retrofit

Dome shell placed over existing single-story structure, creating a double-envelope.
- Buffer zone acts as evaporative cooling intake
- Winter solar gain collector
- Structural loading on existing walls must be evaluated

### 3. Greenfield Village Clusters

5–15 dome units around shared thermal chimney and aquaponics core.
- Shared infrastructure reduces per-unit cost
- Layout follows permaculture zone design principles
- Best opportunity for full integrated system validation

---

## Structural Note

**Rooftop installations require professional structural assessment.** The dome shell weight of 15,000–20,000 kg is a significant load on any existing structure. Do not proceed without qualified engineering review.
