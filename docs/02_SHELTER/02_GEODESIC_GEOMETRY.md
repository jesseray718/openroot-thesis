# Geodesic Geometry — Section 4.2

> ⚠️ **THEORETICAL** — Geodesic dome structural behavior is well-established. The specific AeroCement shell structural analysis requires prototype load testing.

*From THESIS.md Section 4.2*

---

## Why Geodesic?

1. **Structural efficiency** — Triangulated surface distributes loads through membrane action, minimizing bending moments
2. **Surface-to-volume ratio** — Minimizes material use and heat transfer surface relative to enclosed volume
3. **Self-supporting thin shell** — Enables 50–75mm shell thickness target
4. **Wind resistance** — Curved form deflects wind loads efficiently

---

## Frequency Selection

**3V geodesic** recommended for domes under 8m diameter. Higher frequency (4V, 5V) increases structural efficiency but adds construction complexity.

**6m diameter 3V geodesic dome:**
- 80 triangular panels
- 3 strut lengths (a, b, c)
- 35 vertices

---

## Hub Connection

**Proposed:** Cast AeroCement hub with embedded stainless steel connecting plates.
- Hub radius: 75mm
- Connection: stainless bolt through cast insert
- **Adhesive-only connections are prohibited** (see Hard Constraints)

---

## Base Tension Ring

Geodesic domes generate outward horizontal thrust at the base ring.

**Design thrust (6m dome, 1.5 kPa live load):**
```
H_thrust ≈ q × r² / (2 × h) ≈ 1,500 × 9 / (2 × 3) = 2,250 N/m
```

Base ring must resist **2.25 kN/m hoop tension minimum.**  
Use: continuous 50mm × 6mm steel flat bar OR 100mm cast AeroCement ring beam.
