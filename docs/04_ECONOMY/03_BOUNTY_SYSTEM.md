## Research Bounties (THERM tokens)

These bounties reward validated measurements that advance the scientific basis of the AeroCement system.

| Bounty | Reward |
|--------|--------|
| Validate stack effect at 4m chimney height, 35°C ambient, 6m dome | 5,000 THERM |
| Characterize AeroCement mix (fly ash 60%/metakaolin 40%) thermal conductivity | 3,000 THERM |
| Quail tower waste nitrogen yield per 100 birds at 90-day measurement | 2,000 THERM |
| Full 12-month interior temperature log (instrumented dome) | 8,000 THERM |
| Aquaponics water quality stability study (6 months minimum) | 4,000 THERM |

## Claiming Bounties

1. Open a GitHub Issue with your bounty claim, linking to your data/code/translation
2. Technical Council reviews and confirms methodology
3. Build Validators confirm on-site work where applicable
4. Tokens issued upon multi-sig confirmation
