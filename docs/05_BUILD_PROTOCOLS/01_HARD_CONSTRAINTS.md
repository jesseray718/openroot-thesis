# Hard Constraints — Section 6.1

> These are **non-negotiable** design limits. Builds violating these constraints are not AeroCement-compliant and cannot earn BUILD tokens.

*From THESIS.md Section 6.1*

---

## Structural Constraints

1. **Minimum shell thickness:** 50mm for spans <6m; 75mm for 6–9m spans
2. **Base ring continuity:** Tension ring must be continuous and closed — no gaps
3. **Penetration framing:** All penetrations (windows, doors, chimney) must maintain continuity of tension ring
4. **Hub connections:** Must include positive mechanical lock (bolt or wedge). **Adhesive-only connections are prohibited.**
5. **Foundation bearing:** Must bear on undisturbed soil or engineered fill. No bearing on made ground without geotechnical assessment.

## Thermal Constraints

6. **Chimney cross-section:** Minimum 0.15 m² for 4–6m dome diameter
7. **Evaporative media depth:** Minimum 100mm at design airflow velocity
8. **Thermal mass minimum:** 200 kg/m² of floor area
9. **Glazing:** All windows must be double-glazed or double-film. **Single-pane is prohibited** in climate zones with >40°C ambient.

## Water Constraints

10. **Drainage:** All water systems must have overflow and drainage directed away from foundation
11. **Aquaponics bypass:** Must have emergency bypass to prevent fish loss during pump failure
12. **Potable water reserve:** Minimum 200L/person emergency storage

## Biological Constraints

13. **No pesticide application** within 10m of aquaponics or seed bank
14. **Waste treatment:** Composting toilet or equivalent required. No flush connection to aquaponics without triple-stage treatment.
