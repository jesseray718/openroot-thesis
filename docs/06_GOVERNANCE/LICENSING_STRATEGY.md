---

## Why ShareAlike?

The ShareAlike clause is the key provision. It means:
- Anyone who builds on this work must share their improvements under the same license
- No one can take this design, make modifications, and lock those modifications away from the community
- The knowledge commons grows — it cannot be enclosed

This is by design. The goal is permanent open access.
